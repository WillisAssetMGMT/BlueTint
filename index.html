Pro Tips
  • Book escape rooms & upscale restaurants well in advance — they fill up fast on weekends.
  • Verify the Astros home schedule at mlb.com/astros before choosing your itinerary.
  • June in Houston = 95°F+ and humid. Schedule all outdoor activities for early morning or after 6pm.
  • Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is easy and affordable across all these neighborhoods. Skip the rental car stress.
  • CityPASS bundles Space Center + HMNS + Zoo + 2 more attractions for ~$89 — saves ~52%.
  • The Farmers Market is Saturday-only (June 27). Plan around it if you want that experience.
